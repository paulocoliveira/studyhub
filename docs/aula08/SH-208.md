# SH-208 — Quick Complete from Content Listing

## Status

Ready for Development

## User Story

As a user, I want to mark content as completed directly from the content listing so I can update my learning progress without opening the content detail page.

## Refined Behaviour

Allow users to mark content as Completed directly from the content listing without opening the content detail page.

## Refinement Decisions

- Content with status New or In Progress can be marked as Completed directly from the listing.
- The Quick Complete action must be available in both Card View and List View.
- The status change to Completed must be persisted.
- When Completed content is hidden according to the existing visibility rule, the item must be removed from the listing after the action.
- When Show Completed is active, the item must remain visible and display the Completed status.
- Content that is already Completed must not display the Quick Complete action.
- The action must respect the existing content ownership rules.
- When applicable, the current listing context must be preserved after the action, including search, filters, sorting, pagination, and the Show Completed state.
- The action must use a CSRF-protected POST request.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A user can Quick Complete New and In Progress content from Card View and List View.
2. After Quick Complete, the content has status Completed when the listing is refreshed.
3. With Completed content hidden, a Quick Completed item no longer appears in the listing.
4. With Show Completed active, a Quick Completed item remains in the listing with a Completed status.
5. Completed content does not present the Quick Complete action.
6. A user cannot Quick Complete content they do not own.
7. The listing preserves its applicable search, filters, sorting, pagination, and Show Completed state after the action.
8. Quick Complete is submitted through a CSRF-protected POST request.

## Relevant Requirements

- FR-16 — Content statuses
- FR-17 — Content listing
- FR-22 — Card/List views
- FR-27 — Completed visibility
- FR-54 — Quick Complete from Content Listing
- US-15 — Existing status change behaviour
- US-40 — Quick Complete from Content Listing

## Out of Scope

- Undoing Completed directly from the listing.
- Changing content directly to New or In Progress.
- Bulk complete.
- A confirmation modal.
- An AJAX update without a page reload.
